# Field study: garden water use

A small garden compared two irrigation schedules over three days.

## 1. Recorded water use

| Day | Drip (L) | Sprinkler (L) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | 12 | 18 |
| Tuesday | 10 | 17 |
| Wednesday | 11 | 19 |
| Total | 33 | 54 |

The drip schedule used 33 L in total. The sprinkler schedule used 54 L.

## 2. Observations

Soil moisture was checked each morning before watering.

The comparison describes this garden only; it is not a general recommendation.

## 3. Follow-up

Repeat over a full week and record rainfall.
